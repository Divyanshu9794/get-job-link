# 🚀 GetJobLink

## Clean Daily Tech Jobs | Direct Hiring Links for Developers

[![Website](https://img.shields.io/badge/Website-getjoblink.in-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://getjoblink.in/)
[![React](https://img.shields.io/badge/React.js-Frontend-61DAFB?style=for-the-badge&logo=react)](https://react.dev/)
[![Vite](https://img.shields.io/badge/Vite-Build_Tool-646CFF?style=for-the-badge&logo=vite)](https://vite.dev/)

**GetJobLink** is a tech job discovery website designed to help freshers, developers, and experienced professionals find relevant technology and engineering opportunities through direct company hiring links.

The platform brings job opportunities together in one place, helping job seekers discover openings without unnecessary recruiter spam or application fees.

🌐 **Live Website:** https://getjoblink.in/

---

## ✨ Features

### 💼 Job Discovery
- Daily technology and engineering job opportunities.
- Full-time and internship openings.
- Search and filter jobs by company, domain, experience, location, and work type.
- Discover remote and location-based opportunities.
- Direct company application links.
- Detailed job descriptions, required skills, and eligibility information.
- Regularly updated job listings.

### 📄 Resume Builder
- Create professional resumes.
- Organize skills, experience, education, and projects.
- Build ATS-friendly resumes.

### 🎯 ATS Score Evaluator
- Evaluate resumes for ATS compatibility.
- Identify potential resume improvement areas.
- Improve resume structure and keyword relevance.

### 📊 Hiring Radar
- Explore hiring trends and job opportunities.
- Discover hiring activity and relevant opportunities.
- Get insights to make job searching more informed.

### 🔔 Feedback & Suggestions
- Share feedback directly through the website.
- Report issues and suggest new features.
- Help improve the platform through community participation.

### 🔐 Admin Dashboard
- Manage and publish job listings.
- Review and maintain job data.
- Manage job sources and configurations.
- Monitor website activity and analytics.
- Track platform engagement and performance.

## 🎯 Our Mission

Our mission is to make technology job discovery simpler, more transparent, and accessible.

We want job seekers to spend less time searching across multiple platforms and more time preparing for the right opportunities.

**No unnecessary spam. No application fees. Just opportunities.**
